# TransmuteAI: Demo Video Guide (Max 2 Minutes)

> **Target Video Length:** 1 Minute 50 Seconds (~110 seconds)  
> **Target Audience:** Hackathon Judges, Enterprise Architects, Technical Evaluators  
> **Format:** Screen Recording with Voiceover Narration  

---

## ⏱️ Scene-by-Scene Timed Script

### Scene 1: The Problem & Value Proposition (0:00 - 0:20 | 20s)
- **Visual:** Open on the TransmuteAI Dashboard (`http://localhost:3000`). Show the sleek dark slate UI with the live Gemini and Blockchain status badges.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Enterprise AI document generation is broken. When organizations generate slide decks, executive summaries, video scripts, and social posts independently, models hallucinate conflicting facts, and confidential PII leaks across systems.
  > Welcome to TransmuteAI — a platform that enforces 100% semantic consistency across 7 deliverables using a canonical intermediate JSON schema and cryptographic blockchain provenance."

---

### Scene 2: Multi-Modal Ingestion & PII Redaction (0:20 - 0:45 | 25s)
- **Visual:** Click the "Enterprise AI Security Audit" sample button (or drop a PDF/DOCX file). Highlight the instant extraction and the green audit badge: `"4 PII Redactions Applied"`.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Step 1: Multi-Modal Ingestion. We upload or drop our document — whether it's a PDF, DOCX, or image contract scan. 
  > Before prompting any AI model, our deterministic perimeter engine scrubs emails, phone numbers, SSNs, credit cards, and IP addresses, generating an immutable audit log. Our sensitive data never leaks."

---

### Scene 3: Canonical Content Brief JSON intermediate (0:45 - 1:10 | 25s)
- **Visual:** Click **"Synthesize Content Brief JSON"**. Show the Content Brief Inspector appearing with its multi-format tabs. Click between "Video Package", "LinkedIn Post", "Strategic Advisory", and "Infographic".
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Step 2: Canonical Synthesis. Using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, TransmuteAI converts the raw content into a single source of truth — the Content Brief JSON.
  > Here you see a complete Video Storyboard with timestamps and camera directions, a human-grade LinkedIn thought leadership post, a McKinsey-style Strategic Advisory memo with a risk matrix, and infographic layout specs. Because they all originate from this single JSON schema, facts, numbers, and recommendations match with 100% precision."

---

### Scene 4: Parallel Multi-Format Generation & Blockchain Provenance (1:10 - 1:40 | 30s)
- **Visual:** Select all 7 format checkboxes and click **"Dispatch Parallel Generation"**. Watch the progress bar move to 100%. Show the generated deliverable download cards appearing (DOCX, PPTX, Video Package + SRT, LinkedIn, Advisory, Infographic, QR Code).
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Step 3: Parallel Synthesis. We select our desired formats and click Dispatch. Our execution engine compiles every deliverable simultaneously.
  > Here is our executive Word summary with embedded QR codes, our widescreen 16:9 presentation deck, our full video production package with broadcast subtitles, and our strategic advisory.
  > Simultaneously, every single file is SHA-256 fingerprinted and anchored to our cryptographic blockchain ledger."

---

### Scene 5: Blockchain Explorer & Verification (1:40 - 1:50 | 10s)
- **Visual:** Click **"Blockchain Ledger"** in the top navigation. Show the interactive modal displaying Block #1, the hash chain, and paste a hash into the search bar to demonstrate instant green cryptographic verification.
- **Voiceover:**
  > "Any stakeholder can scan the embedded QR code or query our ledger API to verify that the deliverable is authentic and tamper-free.
  > TransmuteAI: Verified, multi-modal, enterprise-grade AI transformation. Thank you."

---

## 🎬 Recording Tips for Evaluators:
1. **Screen Resolution:** Record at 1920x1080 (1080p) or 2560x1440.
2. **Audio:** Use a clean USB microphone or headset; speak with brisk, confident pacing.
3. **Tools:** OBS Studio, Loom, or Windows Game Bar (`Win + G`).
